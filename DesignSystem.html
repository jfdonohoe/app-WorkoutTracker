Timer states

Rest bar (RestTimerBar)

Between exercises, on the in-progress screen. A faint container with the timer fill revealed left→right as the rest elapses; the type is dark over the container and flips to white where the fill has passed. Controls below it: Pause/Resume, Reset, +30s, Done, bordered in muted foreground.
